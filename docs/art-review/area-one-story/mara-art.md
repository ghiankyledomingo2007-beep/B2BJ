# Mara — art record

Created 2026-10-23 with the built-in imagegen tool. The original generated transparent PNG is preserved unchanged at `assets/source/characters/mara_sheet.generated.png`, outside the packaged game. The left square supplies `assets/characters/npcs/mara_portrait.png`, imported offline to 64 × 64 with fifteen colors and hard transparency, then drawn at 2×. User review rejected the original right-hand standing sprite; [the polish record](polish-art.md) documents its replacement. No image generation runs during play.

Existing Rainoray and Warden assets were reused for their portraits.

## Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: one transparent pixel-art character sheet for a dark-fantasy top-down RPG, character Mara, a weary but capable field medic. Two equal square cells side by side, horizontal 2:1 canvas. LEFT CELL: a bust portrait, three-quarter facing slightly right, face readable and calm, dark brown tied-back hair with a single grey streak, warm medium brown skin, brown eyes, weathered muted moss-green hood pushed back, cream linen scarf, small brass expedition clasp, worn brown shoulder satchel strap. RIGHT CELL: matching single full-body standing front/down-facing overworld sprite, viewed from a slightly elevated top-down RPG angle, boots together, arms resting, modest practical long green coat and cream scarf, small medical satchel at her hip. No weapons, no magic. Strict low-resolution hand-placed pixel-art look: each square cell is a 64 by 64 logical pixel canvas enlarged exactly 8 times using nearest-neighbor; crisp square pixels, dark ink outlines, restrained 16-color palette, hard stepped highlights, no smooth shading or anti-aliasing. Portrait centered with 4 logical pixels of transparent margin; full body occupies 30 logical pixels wide and 52 logical pixels tall centered in right cell, all feet and head included. Both cells separated only by transparent space. Transparent background, no scenery, no shadow outside the figure, no UI, no border, no letters, no labels, no text, no watermark. Export a single 1024 by 512 transparent PNG if possible.

## Review

The generated image is 1774 × 887, with transparent margins and matching portrait/sprite halves. Screenshots beside this file show the integrated game renderer, not a UI mockup.
