Resource and balance proposal

Ichor keeps human form active. Parry and charged cleave would stop charging extra Ichor; the ultimate would use one earned readiness charge, stored across form changes. Keep that readiness in the existing RMB slot. Beelzebub stores at most one empowerment, consumed by the next charged cleave or ultimate.

Preparation and recovery stay vulnerable. Only the advancing part of the ultimate grants brief protection. Bosses need explicit parryable attacks and bounded stagger. Readiness gain, damage, cooldowns and enemy openings still need implementation and playtesting.

Current game scenery and sprites, with synthesized void-magic sounds; staged choreography and proposed effects at 60 fps. The parry opening is extended for the draft. Ultimate movement, hits and empowerment are prototypes, not a working replacement skill. Two silhouette frames mark each major contact; the reduced-effects option removes the added flashes and shake for all three clips. Voices and music are omitted to hear the attacks clearly.
