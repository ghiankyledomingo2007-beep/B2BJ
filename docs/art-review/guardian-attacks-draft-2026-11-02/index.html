My take: the model already sells an armored guardian. The attacks need clearer weight and different decisions. A front sweep should leave its back safe; a faultline should travel through the ground; a missed slam should leave a real opening.

Violet armor, horn-gold edges and cyan core energy now carry both phases. Phase I contains the power; phase II exposes the seams and suspends broken armor around the body.
