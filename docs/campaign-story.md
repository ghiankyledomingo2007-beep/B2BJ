# Blob to Blade: campaign story

Source: *Blob to Blade Game Concept Document*, v1.0, August 5, 2026,
sections 3.4 and 4.1–4.4. The Java game remains the implementation.
Rainoray, the four biome names, his former life, the Ichor premise, the final
Ichor Golem, and the two endings come from the GCD. The supporting cast,
local history, intermediate boss names, dialogue, and optional quests below
are original connective material. No generated visuals are required.

## Premise and delivery

Rainoray died at his desk after years of working past exhaustion. He awakens
as a small cyan slime in Ichor spilled by a defeated expedition. The kingdom's
knights marched toward a demon rift and never returned. Their stolen power
now moves inside the creatures that killed them.

The route follows the expedition from its ruined departure point to the
machine it left running beneath the citadel. Survivors have different reasons
to stay: a medic waits for people to come home, a ferryman keeps a path open,
a keeper remembers the dead, and an engineer maintains an imperfect seal.
They need practical help. None appoints Rainoray king or grants unlimited
power. The blade remains borrowed strength with a visible end.

Each biome has a clear main objective, one named survivor, and an optional
memory to find. Side routes can be explored in either order and revisited.
Optional discoveries alter the survivor's conversation and complete a local
quest; they never make an ending unavailable. The plot folds back at each
boss and at the final rift. Short conversations pause combat. Environmental
notes and the journal carry detail that need not interrupt the critical path.

## 1. Ruined Outpost — the people left behind

Main objective: find the east gatehouse, defeat the Outpost Warden, and take
the road into the Corrupted Forest. The abandoned defenses teach movement,
Ichor collection, and the first safe transformation.

**Mara, field medic.** She wears an expedition sash over patched travel
clothes. She stayed after the evacuation because a wounded soldier could
still arrive. At first she mistakes Rainoray's gold core for a signal lamp.
When he understands her, she begins speaking to him as a person.

First conversation:

> Mara: I nearly hung you on the gate. Thought that glow was a lamp.
>
> Rainoray: Wouldn't be the strangest job I've had.
>
> Mara: Rainoray, then. East gate is barred. The Warden still thinks everyone
> outside the walls is an enemy.

Optional quest, **The Missing Names**: inspect the dispatch at the Signal
Tower and return to Mara. The last expedition roster lists several people
as missing rather than dead. She stops waiting for a formal order to leave
and starts preparing a shelter for whoever returns.

Memory: "The east road stays open until the last stretcher passes. If command
asks why the bell is still ringing, tell them to carry one."

Quest return:

> Mara: No final count. Only names crossed out and written back in.
>
> Rainoray: Keep the names. Leave the door open.
>
> Mara: Then I can do both.

Transition: "The Warden falls. Behind the open gate, Mara lights a lamp for
the road. Ahead, the forest answers with a hundred smaller lights."

## 2. Corrupted Forest — a way through

Main objective: cross the corrupted woods, defeat Briarheart, and open the
descent into the Demon Catacombs. Rift Imps contest broad clearings; the
forest offers looping paths, shelter, and an optional branch.

**Sedge, ferryman and trail keeper.** His boat is stranded on a dry channel.
He marks safe roots with scraps of sail and maintains the route on foot.
He speaks plainly and measures progress by how many people can use a road.

First conversation:

> Sedge: You crossed the outpost? Good. Someone should use all those roads.
>
> Rainoray: Where does this one go?
>
> Sedge: Below. Briarheart has wrapped itself around the stair. I'll mark
> the way back; you make sure there's a way forward.

Optional quest, **A Road for Others**: recover the wayfinder's note from an
overgrown side trail and return to Sedge. It records an evacuation route
missed by the expedition. He adds it to his markings so survivors can move
between the outpost and forest without following Rainoray into battle.

Memory: "The captain took the stone road. We took the watercourse. If the
flags go dark, follow the white cloth. It was never a surrender."

Quest return:

> Sedge: An old watercourse. I know where that comes out.
>
> Rainoray: Can people still use it?
>
> Sedge: Once I clear it. You aren't the only one who can open a road.

Transition: "Briarheart's roots loosen. Sedge ties white cloth above the
stair. Under the earth, an old bell continues to ring."

## 3. Demon Catacombs — the order that never ended

Main objective: reach the bell vault, defeat the Oathkeeper, and climb to
the Rift Citadel. Fallen Knights hold narrow approaches. Their discipline
has survived their purpose; their shield stance requires the blade.

**Ilyra, keeper of names.** An elderly archivist sheltered among the burial
vaults when the surface fell. She identifies the expedition's dead from
damaged tags and refuses to reduce them to the Ichor they left behind.

First conversation:

> Ilyra: That light knows these halls. You do not.
>
> Rainoray: I'm not one of your knights.
>
> Ilyra: I know. They were ordered to hold until relieved. The Oathkeeper
> still rings the bell. No one ever sent the relief.

Optional quest, **An Order Released**: find the relief order in a sealed
side archive and bring it to Ilyra. The order was written but never carried.
Her record gains the truth: the knights were abandoned, not deserters.

Memory: "Relief approved. Courier unavailable. Hold this order until the
roads are secured. By then, the bell had already stopped answering."

Quest return:

> Ilyra: They were meant to come home.
>
> Rainoray: Will you write that down?
>
> Ilyra: Every name. Someone should have told them while they could hear.

Transition: "The Oathkeeper's bell goes quiet. Ilyra opens a fresh page.
Above the vaults, the citadel shines with all the light the kingdom lost."

## 4. Rift Citadel — what remains yours

Main objective: cross the failing wards, defeat the Ichor Golem, then
interact with the rift to choose its fate. The citadel combines the earlier
enemy types. The Golem's drain aura makes the cost of each blade window
visible during the final fight.

**Orin, ward engineer.** A former expedition technician, kept alive by a
maintenance recess and a stubborn routine. He built part of the sealing
apparatus, but the Golem now draws its Ichor toward the tear. He can explain
the two possible outcomes without selecting one for Rainoray.

First conversation:

> Orin: The Golem was meant to hold our Ichor. Now it feeds the tear.
>
> Rainoray: Can it still be closed?
>
> Orin: Break the Golem first. Then spend the core to close it for good,
> or keep the core and use the old seal. That seal will need people here.

Optional quest, **A Watch Shared**: recover the maintenance record from the
outer ward and return to Orin. It describes how several ordinary keepers
can maintain the seal in shifts. No hidden third ending or stronger power
is unlocked; the discovery supplies a concrete future for the human ending.

Memory: "Four keepers. Six hours each. Never bind one heart to the whole
day. The ward needs a watch, not a martyr."

Quest return:

> Orin: We designed shifts. I had forgotten that part.
>
> Rainoray: Start with the part where you stop.
>
> Orin: When the next keeper arrives. I'll ask Mara to send one.

## Final choice and endings

Both choices appear only after the Ichor Golem falls and Rainoray reaches
the rift. The interface states the consequence before the choice is made.
The choice spends the story's accumulated core, not a required amount of
combat-meter Ichor; reverting during the boss cannot block the ending.

**Close the rift — remain a slime.** Rainoray pours the accumulated core
into the tear and closes it permanently. He survives in slime form. The
survivors reopen roads through the kingdom. At Mara's shelter, someone has
left a low place beside the fire. He rests there; nobody hands him a sword.

Ending text: "The tear closes. The borrowed armor falls away. Rainoray is
still here. At the outpost, Mara leaves a place beside the fire. This time,
he stays long enough to feel its warmth."

**Keep the core — reclaim human form.** Rainoray stabilizes a human body
and helps Orin raise the old seal. The rift remains behind it. The survivors
accept a shared watch; its upkeep is an ongoing responsibility. Rainoray
takes a shift and leaves when the next keeper comes. The conclusion keeps
the GCD's imperfect seal without treating wanting a body as a moral failure.

Ending text: "The seal holds. Rainoray stands on human feet. Beyond the
ward, the tear still turns. When another keeper arrives, he places the
watch key in their hand and walks into the morning."

The optional quests change each survivor's return conversation. Both
outcomes complete the campaign. Neither demands all optional discoveries.

## Runtime binding

`CampaignStory` owns narrative text, one four-line dialogue panel, and the
quest flags `talked_N`, `memory_N`, and `quest_N` for biome indices 0–3.
The game owns travel, combat, checkpoint saves, shard rewards, and final
choice selection. Each area's NPC stands at its hub; its first optional
landmark holds the memory. Other optional landmarks remain exploration.

`talk(int biome)` opens dialogue and returns `true` only on a new quest
completion. `discover(int biome)` opens the memory on its first discovery.
Finding a memory before meeting its NPC is valid. `advanceDialogue()`
closes the panel. `questFlags()` returns an immutable save snapshot;
`restoreQuestFlags(Set<String>)` validates it before changing live state.
Completed quest flags restore their discovered/met prerequisites.

`objective(int biome, boolean bossDefeated)` supplies the current main
guidance; `questObjective(int biome)` supplies optional-quest guidance.
`transitionLines(int biome)` describes first arrival in the requested
biome. The static ending text is available for either `Ending.CLOSE_RIFT`
or `Ending.HUMAN_FORM`; the game permits selection only at the cleared
final rift, regardless of combat Ichor or optional quest completion.
